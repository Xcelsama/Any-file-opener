AnyFile Viewer opens nearly any file type directly on your device — documents, spreadsheets, images, audio, video, archives, code, and more — without uploading anything anywhere.

Features:
* View and edit text, code, and Markdown files with syntax highlighting
* Open Word, Excel, and CSV files
* Browse ZIP archives
* View images and PDFs
* Play audio and video, with basic editing
* Compare two files with a built-in diff viewer
* Transfer files between your own devices via QR code

Everything runs locally. Files are processed entirely on your device — there is no server, no account, and no analytics.
